Delete your Nutrolix account

You can request deletion of your Nutrolix customer account and the personal information associated with it using the form below. This page is available to both app users and people who have uninstalled the app.

To protect your account, send the request using the WhatsApp mobile number registered with your Nutrolix account. Our support team may contact you to verify that you own the account before processing deletion.

Submitting opens your email app with a pre-filled deletion request. You must send the email to complete the request. If your email app does not open, email support@nutrolix.com with the subject “Nutrolix Account Deletion Request” and your registered mobile number.

What will be deleted?

  • Your Nutrolix customer account/profile and associated login identity.
  • Saved delivery addresses and other account profile information associated with your account.
  • Other personal information that is not required to be retained for a lawful business purpose.

What may be retained?

Order, payment, invoice, tax, refund, dispute, fraud-prevention and accounting records may be retained where required by applicable law or for legitimate legal and security purposes. Such records will be restricted and retained only for the applicable retention period.

Processing time

We will acknowledge your request and process verified deletion requests within 30 days, unless additional verification is needed or a longer period is permitted or required by law. If some records must be retained, we will explain the applicable reason.

For help, contact support@nutrolix.com.
